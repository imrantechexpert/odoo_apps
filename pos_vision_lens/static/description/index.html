Take the lens prescription at the counter

A clear card for the right eye and the left eye, saved on the POS order.

Optical shops should not type a prescription into a note. Mark a product as a vision lens. When the cashier sells it, a card opens: SPH, CYL, AXIS, visual acuity, advise, and a signature. The same card is on the order afterwards.

Built for the way a shop actually sells

One tap on the product

Tick Vision Lens on the product. Frames and accessories stay normal. Only lenses open the card.

Two eyes, easy to tell apart

Right eye and left eye sit in separate boxes. SPH and CYL step by 0.25. Visual acuity is a short list.

Still there after the sale

The prescription is stored on the order line. Open the POS order later and read the same card.

The prescription card

On the Point of Sale

  • The card opens as soon as a vision lens is added.
  • SPH runs from -20.00 to +20.00, in steps of 0.25.
  • CYL runs from -6.00 to +6.00, in steps of 0.25.
  • Minus and plus buttons walk one step at a time.
  • Visual acuity: 6/6, 6/9, 6/12, 6/18, 6/24, 6/36, 6/60, CF, HM.
  • AXIS stays free to type. Advise is a note. The customer signs on the screen.
  • A Prescription button reopens the card.

Your lists, not a fixed table

Point of Sale, Configuration, Prescription Values. Add a power, remove one, or archive it. The POS dropdowns and the minus and plus buttons follow that list after the session is reopened. Old orders keep the value that was saved.

The order keeps the card

Each lens line has its own prescription, so two pairs on one ticket do not share one card. The order list shows a short summary. The order form has a Prescriptions page with both eyes, the advise, and the signature.

A receipt a customer can read

Logo on the left. Company name, address, and phone beside it. Then the order number, date and time, customer name, and phone. Each line shows the product, the quantity, and the price. The slip ends with a thank-you line.

Start in a few minutes

  1. Install POS Vision Lens on Odoo 20 with Point of Sale.
  2. Open a lens product and tick Vision Lens. It is also on the Point of Sale tab of the product.
  3. Open the POS session again so the new lists load.
  4. Sell the lens. Fill the card. Apply.
  5. Open the paid order in the backend when you need the card again.

Point of Sale managers edit the SPH, CYL, and visual acuity lists. Cashiers only pick from them.
