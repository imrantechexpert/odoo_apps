Stop the sale when the shelf is empty

Point of Sale for Odoo 20. Products and variants.

A cashier can tap a product that has nothing left, or pick a variant that is already gone. This module opens a clear window and leaves the ticket unchanged. Raising the quantity past what the shop still has is stopped the same way.

The window on the product screen

What the cashier sees

  • The product name, including the variant.
  • The free quantity in this shop.
  • What is already on the open tickets of this screen.
  • The quantity that was just requested.
  • A single button to close the window and keep selling.

Replace popup.png with a capture from your own Point of Sale before you publish.

Built for the way a counter sells

The variant, not the template

Size and color are checked on their own. One variant can be sold while another variant of the same product is blocked.

The shop’s own stock

The quantity is the free quantity in the source location of this Point of Sale, not a company-wide total from another warehouse.

Checked again at payment

If two screens try to take the last unit, the second payment is refused. The stock cannot be driven below zero from the till.

One tick per shop

The block is on when you install the module. Turn it off for a single Point of Sale under Point of Sale → Configuration → Settings → Product & PoS categories → Block out-of-stock sales. Reload the Point of Sale after you change it.

What stays allowed

  • Services, and goods that do not track inventory.
  • Refunds, and a negative quantity.
  • A product that still has free quantity in this shop.
  • Barcode, search, and the product grid all use the same check.
  • A combo is checked on the items inside it.
  • Works with Odoo 20 Point of Sale and Inventory.
